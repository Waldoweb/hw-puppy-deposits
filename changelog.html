16/07/2026 - Version 5.0.0

Fork:
  • Forked from WooCommerce Deposits 4.6.10 (Webtomizer) and rebranded as Puppy Deposits by Hostetler Web Design. Internal class names, namespace and the woocommerce-deposits text domain are intentionally unchanged, so existing product meta and settings carry over untouched.
Payments & Gateways:
  • New gateway-agnostic express/wallet guard: when a deposit is in the cart, PayPal smart buttons and Apple Pay / Google Pay are hidden on the product, cart, mini-cart and checkout, so express checkout can no longer bypass order substitution and charge the full amount. Hooks each gateway's own disable filter (official PPCP, Payment Plugins PayPal, Payment Plugins Stripe) and no-ops when a gateway is absent.
  • Fixed PayPal (PPCP) rejecting deposit payments. PPCP priced its PayPal order from the cart, which knows nothing about the deposit, so PayPal was quoted the full total and the later amount patch failed with a 422. The not-yet-due balance is now surfaced through PPCP's own hook, so the PayPal order is created at the deposit amount.
  • Removed the legacy PPCP and PPEC compatibility shims, superseded by the above.
Order Lifecycle & Emails:
  • New "Fully Paid" order status, distinct from Completed, so a settled deposit order is no longer indistinguishable from a normal completed order. Counts as paid for revenue and analytics, and reduces stock and coupon usage on transition.
  • New customer "Fully Paid" email, sent when the final balance is paid.
  • Fixed the admin and customer "partial payment" emails wrongly firing on the final payment as well as on intermediate ones.
  • Fixed an assignment used in place of a comparison in the order status logic (= where === was meant).
Product & Cart Display:
  • Reskinned the product deposit controls as a "reservation" card, with the original candy toggle switch kept as a selectable alternative to the flat radio layout.
  • Consolidated the cart and checkout totals into a single Total row reading "deposit (of full)" with a balance-due subline, replacing the duplicated Due Today / Balance rows.
  • Rebuilt the cart line-item deposit display; removed the duplicate output on classic themes.
  • Added a Deposit & balance summary (order total, amount paid, balance remaining) to order-received and My Account order views.
  • Added a Balance column to My Account › Orders.
  • When a deposit is forced, the unavailable "Pay in Full" option now reads as clearly disabled and explains why via a tooltip, instead of being greyed by an unrelated theme rule.
  • Fixed the reservation heading flashing bold then thin on load, caused by an external script stripping inline font-weight; the weight is now injected as a real CSS rule.
  • Deduplicated the injected deposit CSS, which was printing three times per page (5,759 → 2,908 bytes).
New Styling Tab:
  • New Styling tab makes the deposit card themeable per site rather than hardcoded. Card background, border, radius, padding and heading styles, plus per-mode controls for the radio and toggle presentations.
  • Every colour and size field accepts a CSS variable such as var(--action), a static value, or calc(), so the deposit UI can inherit a theme's own design tokens. Inputs are validated to block CSS injection. Blank leaves the previous default, so the look is unchanged until customised.
  • Deposit Option Style is now a dropdown (Radio Buttons / Toggle Switch); the admin shows only the relevant controls for the selected mode.
Settings:
  • New "Excluded product categories" setting: products in the chosen categories do not show the deposit option. Child categories are excluded along with their parent, and a product with deposits explicitly enabled in its own settings is unaffected.
  • "Disable Deposits" is now an Enable/Disable dropdown. Selecting Disable greys out the settings it turns off and shows a "Deposits are disabled" badge on every tab. Note that this switch disables all front-end deposit functionality including Checkout Mode; existing partially paid orders keep their reminders and pay links.
  • Renamed "Site-wide Settings" to "Global Settings" and moved it to the top of the General tab; moved Default Selection into Product Deposit Settings.
  • New "Deposit Option Balance Text" setting for the balance subline (blank hides it).
  • Defaulted partially paid orders to editable, and stock reduction to the deposit payment.
Removed:
  • Envato updater, the Automatic Updates tab and purchase-code verification.
  • Admin reports and the WooCommerce Bookings compatibility module.
  • Payment plans, disabled at every entry point; fixed and percentage deposits are kept.
  • The non-functional debugging-mode badge and an unreachable, empty Advanced tab inherited from upstream.
Retained:
  • PDF Invoices & Packing Slips (WP Overnight) compatibility, and the partial-payment engine.
